# Campus Wi-Fi Issue Guide

This is general troubleshooting guidance, not a substitute for official campus policy or an emergency response.

When reporting a campus Wi-Fi issue, include:

- The campus building and approximate room or area.
- The device type and whether other nearby devices are affected.
- When the issue started and any error shown.
- Whether reconnecting or restarting Wi-Fi changed the behavior.

Use CampusConnect's **IT and network** ticket category to send the details to support and track follow-up. Do not include account passwords or one-time codes in the ticket.
